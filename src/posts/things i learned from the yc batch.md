i technically did only half of it, so this is only half the possible learnings of yc!

its shaped the way i think about what i want to do with my life: to be more ambitious and to take on bigger ideas and to operate under the belief that subject-matter expertise can be attained in most fields within 6 months. I used to think that I should do something less ambitious now so I can do something greater in the future, but i think this is a failure mode. cf sam altman, zero to one

it turned out that my social battery was infinite at batch events. im not totally sure on why yet, but probably to do with how interesting and pleasant everyone was to talk to!

people in the batch were unexpectedly chill! i would hang out with most of them even modulo yc; i think yc has a good douchebag detector. not to say there werent any. but on net a very good exp.

execution is at least one oom more important than planning or doing analysis, mainly because valuable information that is most pertinent towards decision making comes only from having made similar adjacent decisions. a tough takeaway for a chud philosophy thinkmaxxer like me, but one i needed
